Our sandbox lets you get familiar with Safepay before your application is processed and approved. This facilitates switching over to to accepting live payments with zero downtime.

## Setting up your sandbox account

To sign up, please visit our [website](https://www.getsafepay.pk/sign-up). Note that your sandbox and live accounts are separate and also need to be created individually.

<Callout type="warn">
Error signing in?

You may have already signed up at [https://getsafepay.com/dashboard/signup](https://getsafepay.com/dashboard/signup) for a live account. You must create a new sandbox account [here](https://sandbox.api.getsafepay.com/dashboard/signup). Note that this is separate from your live account and requires its own sign-up and login.

</Callout>

Create your Safepay account

You will need to provide the following items:

| Item             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Email address    | You may provide any email address. Ideally this email should be from your business domain but we make no restrictions. Your email address must be valid and accessible to you for verification. If you have not received an email from Safepay after signing up please click [here](https://safepay.helpscoutdocs.com/article/42-not-getting-emails-from-safepay). |
| Password         | A strong password is preferred.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Phone number     | Please make sure your phone number is no longer than 10 digits.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Business name    | Ideally your registered business name.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Business website | You may provide a URL. If you do please make sure it is a complete URL e.g. [https://example.com](https://example.com).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

<Callout type="info">
Multiple online stores

If you want to use Safepay Checkout on multiple e-commerce stores (with unique websites), you must create a separate account with Safepay for each of them.

Even if you have been approved for a for one store we treat other stores as unique applications for regulatory reasons. You will have to submit new KYC applications in association with any other stores in order to be approved for multiple Safepay accounts.

Each store will present a unique view for the individual Safepay account only.

</Callout>

## What's next in sandbox?

A Sandbox Account looks and works exactly the same way as a production account except that the money shown in the account is not real and test cards may be used to simulate payments.

You may use your Sandbox Account to:

- View and manage your Sandbox account.
- Use Quick Links to generate one-time payment links.
- View payment details for test payments.
- View notifications generated by your mock transactions.
