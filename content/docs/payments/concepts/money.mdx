Safepay sets certain standards for how money is treated by the API.

# Amounts

Amounts are represented in the lowest denomination (or minor units) of the given currency. This means that PKR is represented in paisas, USD in cents, GBP in pennies and so on.

# Currencies

Currencies are represented by the [ISO 4217](https://www.iso.org/iso-4217-currency-codes.html) currency codes. Safepay supports creating payments in the following currencies:

- `PKR`
- `USD`
- `GBP`
- `EUR`
- `CAD`
- `AED`
- `SAR`
