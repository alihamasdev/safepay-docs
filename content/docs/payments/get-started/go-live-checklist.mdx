Follow the checklist below to ensure you have a complete online payments integration with Safepay.

Before you begin integrating Safepay, make sure you've gone through the following steps:

## Production Account Setup

- You’ve created a **Production Account** via the [Dashboard](https://getsafepay.com/dashboard/login/).
- You've retrieved your **Client-side and Server-side [API keys](/docs/developers/safepay/api-keys)**.
- You've completed your [KYC Onboarding form](https://getsafepay.com/onboarding/login).
- You've been approved for Production access.
- You've gone over the [Integration Checklist](/docs/payments/get-started/integration-checklist)
